## 4. 安全、性能、兼容性、扩展性风险分析与建议
- 安全：本轮没有放宽任何权限或写控逻辑，主要是删除宽松兼容入口，输入面反而更收敛。[RedstoneLinkCrossChunkConfigParser.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/config/RedstoneLinkCrossChunkConfigParser.java#L53) [RedstoneLinkClientDisplayParser.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/config/RedstoneLinkClientDisplayParser.java#L107)

## 5. 并发冲突审查（共享状态、读改写覆盖、重入/线程边界、风险等级与建议）
- `LinkSavedData` 和 `CrossChunkDispatchQueueSavedData` 的共享可变状态仍分别集中在内存 map/set 中，本轮只删除兼容路径，没有新增新的共享容器或异步写入口。[LinkSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkSavedData.java#L53) [LinkSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkSavedData.java#L64) [CrossChunkDispatchQueueSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/CrossChunkDispatchQueueSavedData.java#L59) [CrossChunkDispatchQueueSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/CrossChunkDispatchQueueSavedData.java#L64)
- 读改写覆盖风险主要体现在“旧格式现在直接丢弃”，而不是并发覆盖：`load(...)` 和 `parsePendingEntry(...)` 仍是串行恢复流程，没有引入跨线程竞争窗口。[LinkSavedDataCodecSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkSavedDataCodecSupport.java#L32) [CrossChunkDispatchQueueSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/CrossChunkDispatchQueueSavedData.java#L73) [CrossChunkDispatchQueueCodecSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/CrossChunkDispatchQueueCodecSupport.java#L112)
- 线程边界方面，运行时派发仍在当前主线程语义下把 `PULSE_EVENT/TOGGLE_EVENT` 落到 `DeltaKind.ACTIVATION`，因此“删除旧持久化格式兼容”没有改变 block entity 的内部激活处理边界。[CrossChunkDispatchRuntimeSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/CrossChunkDispatchRuntimeSupport.java#L180) [CrossChunkDispatchRuntimeSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/CrossChunkDispatchRuntimeSupport.java#L191)
