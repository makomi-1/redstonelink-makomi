- GUI 数据流保持原状：服务端仍通过 `RepeaterNetwork` 打开编辑器，客户端 `RepeaterEditorScreen` 读取快照后渲染；本轮只把最终渲染预设切到转发器主题，没有改动编辑保存链路。[RepeaterEditorScreen.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/RepeaterEditorScreen.java#L92) [RepeaterEditorScreen.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/RepeaterEditorScreen.java#L191) [RepeaterEditorScreen.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/RepeaterEditorScreen.java#L361)

- 风险等级：低。本轮新增逻辑全部位于客户端渲染线程侧，没有新开线程，也没有新增全局可变状态；`PanelStyle` 是不可变枚举，`REPEATER` 预设也是静态常量，不存在并发写冲突。[LinkSerialHudOverlayDrawSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/LinkSerialHudOverlayDrawSupport.java#L115) [GuiBackgroundRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiBackgroundRenderSupport.java#L264)
