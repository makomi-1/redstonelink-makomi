## 2. 关键数据结构和算法性能分析
- 自隔离没有新增全局扫描结构，只在原有 `triggerSourceToCores/coreToTriggerSources` 访问路径上做常数级判断；对非转发器边几乎没有额外成本。[LinkSavedDataLinkIndexSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkSavedDataLinkIndexSupport.java#L399) [LinkSavedDataLinkIndexSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkSavedDataLinkIndexSupport.java#L414)
- QTL 频道预览沿用原有“按渲染距离扫描已加载区块 + 按颜色聚合边框”的算法，避免为频道模式再做一套渲染路径；新增开销主要是一次成员序号集合匹配，复杂度仍受客户端已加载区块规模控制。[QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L235) [QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L379)
- 为避免逐帧请求服务端，频道预览增加了“待返回请求 + 已返回成员”双短 TTL 缓存；同键请求在 TTL 内只发一次，同键响应在 TTL 内可复用多帧线框扫描结果。[QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L322) [QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L667) [QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L703) [QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L721)

## 5. 并发冲突审查（共享状态、读改写覆盖、重入/线程边界、风险等级与建议）
- 风险等级：低。服务端新增频道预览接包仍统一切回主线程执行，没有把 `LinkSavedData` 查询暴露到网络线程；客户端接包也统一回到主线程后才写渲染缓存。[QuickLinkNetworkRegistrationSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkRegistrationSupport.java#L82) [QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L48)
- 客户端共享状态只有 `cachedPreviewOutlineState/cachedChannelPreviewState/pendingChannelPreviewRequest` 三个静态快照，读写都位于客户端主线程渲染或接包回调中，未新增跨线程读改写覆盖点；同时在世界丢失或未持有 QTL 时会主动清空，避免跨世界残留引用。[QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L129) [QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L346)
- 自隔离改动不改变现有事件分发线程边界，只是在主线程图写入前后多做合法性裁剪；未引入新的重入路径。建议后续若把频道预览改成更长寿命缓存，再补一次“断线/切维度/换手持物”状态清理审查。[LinkSetExecutionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/command/link/LinkSetExecutionService.java#L221) [LinkSavedDataLinkIndexSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkSavedDataLinkIndexSupport.java#L414)
