- `sync` 真值仍由 `NavigableMap<TimeKey, Map<SourceKey, SyncConcurrentEntry>>` 承载，本次新增的是按时间键裁剪旧桶，而不是全量清空；这样能保留同 tick `sync` 优先级仲裁和更晚 tick 的合法迟到事件。[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L220) [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L630)

- 安全性：本次未引入 `com.makomi.api.v1.*` 依赖，也没有放宽节点活性校验；空别名删除仍只作用于已分配且未退役的当前节点，风险低。[PairingNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/PairingNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L145)

## 5. 并发冲突审查（共享状态、读改写覆盖、重入/线程边界、风险等级与建议）
- 共享状态：`syncConcurrentBuckets`、`runtimeSimulatedSyncConcurrentBuckets`、`pulse/toggle` 快照与 `NodeAliasSavedData` 都属于读改写共享状态，本次改动集中在“先读当前结果，再删旧真值，再重算 authority”的顺序控制。[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L220) [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L417) [PairingNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/PairingNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L166)
- 读改写覆盖：`toggle` 基准仍在清理旧 `sync` 前计算，避免把“对当前状态取反”误算成“对已清空状态取反”；这是本次时序正确性的关键保护点。[ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java#L229) [ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java#L248)
