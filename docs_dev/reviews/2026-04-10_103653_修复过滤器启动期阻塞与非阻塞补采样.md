## 1. 功能与语义、架构设计（关键方法和入口）
- 本轮把过滤器真值更新拆成“无采样恢复”和“带采样刷新”两条路径：`upsertFilter(...)` 只恢复/登记持久化真值，不再主动读取邻居输入；`refreshFilterWithCurrentNeighborSignal(...)` 继续承担运行期即时采样刷新。[LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L42) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L62)
- 过滤器方块实体的生命周期也同步收口：`clearRemoved()` 现在只走 `restorePlacedFilterState()` 恢复放置态真值，配置保存与世界态变化则继续走 `refreshPlacedFilterState()` 采样路径，避免启动附着阶段再做潜在阻塞读取。[AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.java#L78) [AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.java#L97) [AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.java#L182)
- 为了补齐“读档恢复后仍能尽快拿到最新邻居阈值”，`LinkDispatchFilterService.register()` 新增了 `SERVER_STARTED` 后非阻塞补采样入口，只对 `NEIGHBOR_MAX_INPUT` 过滤器条目做一次已加载实例重采样。[LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L31) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L157)

## 2. 关键数据结构和算法性能分析
- `PlacedLinkFilterSavedData` 新增 `upsertPreservingNeighborSignal(...)` 与内部 `upsertInternal(...)`，把“归一化/比较/倒排索引维护”复用到同一条写路径中，避免双份逻辑分叉。[PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java#L101) [PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java#L124)
- `FilterEntry` 暴露了 `dimension/filterKind/filterPos/usesNeighborSignalThreshold` 只读访问器，使启动补采样可以直接以持久化条目为驱动，而不需要重新扫世界或重新解析 NBT，额外成本保持为“条目数线性 + 已加载 chunk 命中常数开销”。[PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java#L361)
- `SERVER_STARTED` 补采样显式使用 `getChunkNow(...) + EntityCreationType.CHECK` 非阻塞探测已加载区块；未加载过滤器直接跳过，不会触发同步等待 chunk 的启动期卡顿。时间复杂度为 O(n) 遍历持久化过滤器条目，单条命中成本局限于一次 `Map`/chunk 查询。[LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L164) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L191)

## 4. 安全、性能、兼容性、扩展性风险分析与建议
- 性能与稳定性方面，本轮核心收益是把启动附着链路从“可能同步读取邻居世界态”改为“只恢复持久化真值”，并把首次阈值刷新后移到 `SERVER_STARTED` 后的非阻塞路径，直接规避了带过滤器存档进档卡死的风险。[AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.java#L182) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L157)
- 兼容性方面，`upsertPreservingNeighborSignal(...)` 会保留旧的 `neighborSignalStrength`，因此重启或区块重附着时不会把上一次有效阈值误写成 0；若过滤器所在区块在 `SERVER_STARTED` 时仍未加载，则该值会暂时沿用旧快照，等后续邻居变化或配置保存再刷新。[PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java#L109) [PlacedLinkFilterSavedDataTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/data/PlacedLinkFilterSavedDataTest.java#L62)
- 扩展性方面，本轮没有改动 `triggerSource/core` 派发方向，也没有引入 `com.makomi.api.v1.*` 依赖；若后续要进一步做“分 tick 预算化启动补采样”或“按维度分批刷新”，当前 `entriesSnapshot()` + `usesNeighborSignalThreshold()` 这组访问器已经提供了最小扩展点。[PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java#L203) [PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java#L394)

## 5. 并发冲突审查（共享状态、读改写覆盖、重入/线程边界、风险等级与建议）
- 共享状态仍集中在 `PlacedLinkFilterSavedData` 的 `entriesByKey/chunkIndex`，本轮没有新增异步线程写入口；`SERVER_STARTED` 回调仍运行在服务端生命周期线程边界内，读改写覆盖模型与既有 `SavedData` 写入一致。风险等级：低。[PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/PlacedLinkFilterSavedData.java#L54) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L34)
- 真正的线程边界改进点在于：启动补采样不再通过 `level.getBestNeighborSignal(...)` 间接带入未知区块加载，而是先用 `getChunkNow(...) + CHECK` 探测就绪性，再决定是否读取 block entity。这样把“是否已加载”作为显式门禁，避免主线程在启动期等待异步 chunk 完成。风险等级：低。[LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L164) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L205)
- 自动化测试新增了两层约束：一层验证无采样恢复会保留旧邻居阈值，另一层验证启动补采样只命中 `NEIGHBOR_MAX_INPUT` 条目。当前未发现新的共享状态覆盖或重入问题；后续若把启动补采样改成跨 tick 队列，再额外复查预算状态的线程边界即可。[PlacedLinkFilterSavedDataTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/data/PlacedLinkFilterSavedDataTest.java#L62) [LinkDispatchFilterServiceTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterServiceTest.java#L12)
