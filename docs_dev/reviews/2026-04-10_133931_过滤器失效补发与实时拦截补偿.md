# 过滤器失效补发与实时拦截补偿

## 1. 功能与语义、架构设计

- 本轮把过滤器运行时变化正式接入 `sync` 失效/补发主链：`LinkDispatchFilterService.refreshFilterWithCurrentNeighborSignal(...)` 在更新持久化真值后，会继续进入 `reconcileSyncDeltaAfterFilterChange(...)`，按过滤器种类分流到 send/receive 差分扫描，再统一由 `publishSyncPathTransition(...)` 发布 `sync` 专用失效或补发事件。[LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L72) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L412) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L595)
- 为避免误伤 `toggle/pulse`，本轮没有复用现有 `TRIGGER_SOURCE_INVALIDATION`，而是新增 `SOURCE_INVALIDATION` 发布入口，专门承载“只清 `sync` 贡献”的过滤器失效语义。[InternalDispatchDeltaEvents.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/InternalDispatchDeltaEvents.java#L260) [InternalDispatchDeltaRuleSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/InternalDispatchDeltaRuleSupport.java#L461)
- 实时 `sync` 派发链也同步补齐了“当前新信号被过滤器拦截时清旧贡献”的补偿：来源端 `forwardLinkedSignal(...)` 会先抓旧 replay 快照，再派发，最后落新快照；send 拦截走来源侧失效补偿，receive 拦截走目标侧逐条失效补偿。[TriggerSourceBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/TriggerSourceBlockEntity.java#L67) [TriggerSourceBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/TriggerSourceBlockEntity.java#L88) [LinkedTargetDispatchService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkedTargetDispatchService.java#L207) [LinkedTargetDispatchService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkedTargetDispatchService.java#L263)
- `clearRemoved()` 路径没有被重新塞入扫描逻辑，过滤器启动恢复仍保持“只恢复真值、不触发世界同步读取”的边界；运行时同步发射器和同步拉杆只改成在派发后统一记录新快照，避免旧快照在补偿前被覆盖。[LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L40) [LinkSyncEmitterBlock.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/LinkSyncEmitterBlock.java#L43) [LinkSyncLeverBlock.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/LinkSyncLeverBlock.java#L140)

## 4. 安全、性能、兼容性、扩展性风险分析与建议

- 安全/语义：本轮新增逻辑严格限定在 `triggerSource -> core` 单向链路，并且只回推 `sync` 贡献，没有新增任何反向写入或对 `toggle/pulse` 的历史补发，因此不会破坏现有方向语义。[LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L431) [InternalDispatchDeltaRuleSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/InternalDispatchDeltaRuleSupport.java#L321)
- 性能：空间索引已经把扫描范围从“全节点表”收敛到“覆盖区块桶”，但过滤器邻居频繁抖动时仍会触发链路展开和事件发布；若后续观测到热点，应在过滤器层再加同 tick 去重或节流。[LinkSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkSavedData.java#L475) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L431)
- 兼容性：旧 attach replay 路径未改用阻塞读取，`clearRemoved()` 边界保持不变，现有启动期“禁止同步邻居读取”的经验约束仍成立；本轮也没有引入 `com.makomi.api.v1.*` 依赖。[LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L40) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L647)
- 扩展性：当前 `SOURCE_INVALIDATION` 已成为“只清 `sync`”的专用事件，后续若要补 `toggle/pulse` 的过滤器补偿，建议继续走显式新增 delta 类型或快照来源，不要复用这次的 `sync` 语义硬套到事件型来源上。[InternalDispatchDeltaEvents.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/InternalDispatchDeltaEvents.java#L260) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L595)

## 5. 并发冲突审查

- 共享状态：本轮新增共享状态主要是 `LinkSavedData.nodeChunkIndex` 与过滤器持久化真值 diff 读取；它们都只在服务端主线程的节点注册/过滤器事件路径下读改写，没有引入新的异步线程访问点。[LinkSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkSavedData.java#L64) [LinkSavedDataSerialSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkSavedDataSerialSupport.java#L68)
- 读改写覆盖：`forwardLinkedSignal(...)` 调整为“先抓旧快照、再派发、最后写入新快照”，目的是避免在同一线程内先写新 replay 快照后丢失旧信号，从而让被拦截补偿拿不到正确的前态；这属于单线程时序覆盖风险的显式修复。[TriggerSourceBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/TriggerSourceBlockEntity.java#L67)
- 重入/线程边界：过滤器变化协调最终仍通过内部 delta 总线进入既有 direct/batch/crosschunk 投影链，没有额外绕过批调度器或跨区块队列；因此线程边界保持在原有服务端主线程和既有异步批语义内。风险等级评估为“中低”，主要压力点仍是主线程事件量而不是并发竞态。[LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L615) [LinkedTargetDispatchService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkedTargetDispatchService.java#L207)
- 建议：若后续继续在过滤器变化里扩大事件类型，优先保持“先本地算 diff、后统一发 delta”的结构，不要在过滤器方块实体回调中直接操作目标实体运行态，以免重新引入跨模块重入和时序耦合。
