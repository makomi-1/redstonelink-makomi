## 3. 数据流或调用链

本轮补写后的关键调用链是：玩家手持 quick-link 站立右键命中过滤器 -> `QuickLinkApplyService.apply(...)` 识别过滤器目标 -> `applyToFilterFromCache(...)` 校验缓存类型、权限和输入长度 -> `AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.applySnapshot(...)` 落过滤器快照 -> `LinkDispatchFilterService.refreshFilterWithCurrentNeighborSignal(...)` 重采样 -> `reconcileSyncDeltaAfterFilterChange(...)` 对 `sync` 做失效/补发协调。这个链路现在已在公开使用说明与机制设计中被显式写出，而不再只存在于实现层或内部说明中。[QuickLinkApplyService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/QuickLinkApplyService.java#L33) [QuickLinkApplyService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/QuickLinkApplyService.java#L135) [AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/AbstractLinkFilterBlockEntity.java#L78) [LinkDispatchFilterService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkDispatchFilterService.java#L72) [使用说明.md](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/docs/使用说明.md#L40) [使用说明.md](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/docs/使用说明.md#L85) [机制设计.md](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/docs/机制设计.md#L204) [机制设计.md](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/docs/机制设计.md#L253)
