- recording 会话现在在注册阶段直接挂接专用 `END_SERVER_TICK` 采样钩子，运行时按会话采样周期写入晚相位样本，而不再读取通用 trace 已记录样本。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L57) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L259)
- 初始化顺序上，把 `StatePanelRecordingSessionService.register()` 和 `StatePanelNetwork.register()` 后移到 `InputPlaybackService`、`ChannelDispatchScheduler`、`CoreDispatchBatchScheduler` 之后，保证 recording 采样晚于输入投递与 scheduler flush，同时 auto-stop 晚于 recording 采样。[RedstoneLink.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/RedstoneLink.java#L59) [RedstoneLink.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/RedstoneLink.java#L66)

## 2. 关键数据结构和算法性能分析

- 新增的核心数据结构是 `ActiveSession -> MountedNode -> RecordingSampleBuffer` 三层：
  - `ActiveSession` 持有录制配置、节点列表与生命周期状态；[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L653)
  - `MountedNode` 负责单节点的 `nextSampleTick` 与样本追加；[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L584)
  - `RecordingSampleBuffer` 负责固定容量 ring buffer 行为，满容量时从头部淘汰旧样本。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L743)
- 运行时复杂度仍然是“活动会话数 × 已挂载节点数”的按 tick 线性扫描；每个到期节点只做一次 `NodeRuntimeProbe.snapshot(...)`，与原 recording 挂 trace 的总量级一致，没有引入额外的跨节点聚合复杂度。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L263) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L718)
- 导出阶段直接从会话内缓冲按时间顺序转成 `RecordedSample`，省掉了之前对 `NodeStateTraceService.readSamples(...)` 的反转步骤，导出路径更直接。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L276) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L632)

## 3. 数据流或调用链

- 录制开始时，`start(...)` 先过滤订阅，再通过 `NodeRuntimeProbe.resolveCurrent(...)` 解析每个节点的探针类型与初始快照，并据此创建 `MountedNode` 与会话内缓冲。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L117) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L319)
- 每个 `END_SERVER_TICK` 晚相位，`onEndServerTick(...)` 遍历活动会话，调用 `ActiveSession.captureDueSamples(...)`，再由 `MountedNode.captureIfDue(...)` 真正落样。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L259) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L718) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L623)
- 停止或自动停止时，`buildRecordingBundle(...)` 直接从会话内缓冲生成 `series`，后续仍沿用原来的 `StatePanelRecordingJsonSupport` 压缩与文件名生成链路，因此网页端读取协议不变。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L188) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L276)

## 4. 安全、性能、兼容性、扩展性风险分析与建议

- 安全性：本轮未新增网络权限入口、命令权限或外部文件读写路径，风险主要限于 recording 结果口径变化，等级低。
- 性能：recording 采样仍是按活动节点逐个抓快照。相比之前“全局 trace + 导出读取”的方案，本轮把成本前移到 recording 自身 tick 钩子，但 recording 只在活动会话期间运行，整体可控。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L259)
- 兼容性：recording bundle 结构、字段名和网页读取逻辑未改，历史 recording 文件继续可读；全局 `NodeStateTraceService` 未改，因此既有 bench/trace 基线保持稳定。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L276)
- 扩展性：当前 `RecordingSampleBuffer` 只保存时间顺序样本，后续若要加“stop 前补尾样本”“slot 细分”“额外录制字段”，优先沿 `MountedNode`/`RecordingSampleBuffer` 扩展，不需要回到全局 trace 层重构。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L743)
- 残余风险：手动 stop 仍发生在任意服务端时机，没有专门补“当前未到 END 的最后一拍”样本；这符合本轮“保持 `sampleEveryTicks` 严格语义”的取舍，但如果后续用户更在意 stop 当刻的最后状态，需要单独增加 stop 前补样本策略。

## 5. 并发冲突审查

- `ACTIVE_SESSIONS_BY_SERVER` 仍是服务端主线程内使用的 `IdentityHashMap`，所有写入点都来自录制开始、结束、断线清理和 `END_SERVER_TICK` 主线程钩子，没有新增跨线程共享状态风险。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L52) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L83) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L167) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L394)
- `MountedNode.nextSampleTick` 与 `RecordingSampleBuffer` 都只在服务端主线程写入，没有与网络线程并发竞争；读取发生在 stop/export 时，也在主线程同步完成，风险低。[StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L589) [StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/StatePanelRecordingSessionService.java#L751)
- 注册顺序调整后，recording 采样、auto-stop、输入投递和 scheduler flush 的相对顺序更明确，降低了“当前 tick 先 stop 再采样”导致的重入/覆盖风险。[RedstoneLink.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/RedstoneLink.java#L63) [RedstoneLink.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/RedstoneLink.java#L66)
