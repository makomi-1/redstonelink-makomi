## 1. 功能与语义、架构设计（关键方法和入口）
- 本次修复把转发器读档时用于判断外显态的 `dispatchedOutputPower` 提前到 `super.loadAdditional(...)` 之前恢复，保证基类登记“加载后静默 blockstate 校正”时拿到的是持久化后的真实已派发输出，而不是默认值 `0`。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L253) [LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L498)
- 转发器的可见 `ACTIVE` 状态继续统一收敛为“跟随已派发输出态”，同步入口和挂起判定入口都复用同一 helper，避免回退到输入侧即时真值语义。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L233) [LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L240) [LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L429)
- `clearRemoved()` 保留“恢复延迟 tick 调度 + 持久化 replay 快照”职责，不再在加载附着阶段直接 `setBlock`，从架构上重新接回既有的 attach 后异步自愈链路。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L215)
- 回归测试同时覆盖了“状态不一致时应挂起”“状态一致时不应重复挂起”以及“`clearRemoved()` 不得重新引入直接 blockstate 同步”三类约束。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntityLoadResyncTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntityLoadResyncTest.java#L45) [LinkRepeaterBlockEntityLoadResyncTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntityLoadResyncTest.java#L60) [LinkRepeaterBlockEntityLoadResyncTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntityLoadResyncTest.java#L75)

## 2. 关键数据结构和算法性能分析
- 运行时新增逻辑仅为一次 `NBT -> int` 的 O(1) 读取和一次已有判定链路的时序前移，没有引入新的集合遍历或额外调度开销。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L257) [LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L498)
- 性能收益主要来自避免在区块/实体加载关键路径直接执行 `level.setBlock(...)`，把可见态修正重新延后到现有生命周期派发阶段处理，降低重进存档时卡在线程阻塞链上的概率。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L215) [LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L433)

## 3. 数据流或调用链
- 读档调用链现在是：`loadAdditional()` 先恢复 `dispatchedOutputPower`，再进入基类 `loadAdditional()`，由基类统一重建派生态并登记 `pendingLoadBlockStateSync`。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L253) [ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L792)
- 后续 attach 生命周期消费阶段由 `LinkNodeLifecycleDispatchEvents.consumeAttachTask()` 触发 `consumePendingLoadBlockStateSync()`，再回调到转发器自己的 `syncBlockStateFromDerivedState()`，完成非阻塞的外显修正。[LinkNodeLifecycleDispatchEvents.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkNodeLifecycleDispatchEvents.java#L164) [LinkNodeLifecycleDispatchEvents.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkNodeLifecycleDispatchEvents.java#L188) [ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L432) [LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L233)
- 单测调用链与运行时语义一致：先构造最小转发器块/实体类型，再写入 `dispatchedOutputPower` NBT，最后断言挂起标记是否符合当前外显态契约。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntityLoadResyncTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntityLoadResyncTest.java#L47) [LinkRepeaterBlockEntityLoadResyncTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntityLoadResyncTest.java#L109)

- 安全性：本次改动未新增网络入口、命令入口或 `com.makomi.api.v1.*` 依赖，影响面限定在转发器实体的持久化恢复路径与测试代码，无额外安全暴露面。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L253)

## 5. 并发冲突审查（共享状态、读改写覆盖、重入/线程边界、风险等级与建议）
- 共享状态：运行时新增读写仅涉及同一 `LinkRepeaterBlockEntity` 的 `dispatchedOutputPower` 恢复时序，没有引入新的全局共享状态或跨实体写回覆盖风险。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L253)
- 线程边界：核心收益在于把原先可能发生在加载附着阶段的直接 `setBlock` 副作用移除，重新回到既有生命周期消费点执行，降低区块加载过程中出现重入/阻塞的风险。[LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/LinkRepeaterBlockEntity.java#L215) [LinkNodeLifecycleDispatchEvents.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkNodeLifecycleDispatchEvents.java#L188)
