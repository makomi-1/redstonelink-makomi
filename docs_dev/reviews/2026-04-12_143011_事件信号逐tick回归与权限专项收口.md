## 4. 安全、性能、兼容性、扩展性风险分析与建议
- 兼容性边界与用户确认一致：`sync` 失效后只回退到仍存在的 `sync`，不会 resurrect 被更晚 `sync` 清掉的事件快照；因此旧的 `toggle fallback` 语义已被主动移除，后续新增测试或说明不能再按旧模型断言。[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L200) [ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L526)

## 5. 并发冲突审查（共享状态、读改写覆盖、重入/线程边界、风险等级与建议）
- 共享状态仍集中在单个 `core` 方块实体内部，读改写对象只有事件快照、`sync` 来源桶与 authority；本轮没有新增跨实体缓存或静态全局状态。风险等级：低。[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L23) [ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L513)
- 读改写覆盖风险的关键修复点是“先读基准、后切 authority”。此前 later `toggle` 会先把 authority 设成 `TOGGLE` 再解当前态，形成同一调用内的自覆盖；现在 direct / batch 两条路径都先取 `baseToggleState`，该风险已消除。[ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L456) [ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java#L230) [ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java#L382)
- 事件域互斥写入已经具备明确串行规则：`pulse` 写入先清 `toggle`，`toggle` 写入先清 `pulse`，`sync` 再按时间清整个事件域，因此不会出现同一 tick 内双事件快照同时长期存活。线程边界未变化，仍是服务端主线程实体更新链路。风险等级：低，残余建议是继续保持所有旁路写入最终都回到同一 dispatch 入口提交。[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L253) [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L376) [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L403)
