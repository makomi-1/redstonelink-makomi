- `sync` 仍保留 `TreeMap<TimeKey, Map<SourceKey, SyncConcurrentEntry>>` 这一类来源聚合结构，用于支持来源级失效、replay 和最大强度重算；这部分复杂度没有扩张，仍是按有效来源数做聚合扫描。[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L34) [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L320)

## 4. 安全、性能、兼容性、扩展性风险分析与建议
- 兼容性：持久化辅助仍保留旧 `pulse/toggle` 并发条目的读取回退，但新写回只写事件快照键，符合“读旧写新”的兼容边界。[ActivatableTargetPersistenceHelper.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetPersistenceHelper.java#L150) [ActivatableTargetPersistenceHelper.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetPersistenceHelper.java#L166)
- 语义风险已明显下降：此前 `PULSE` 对 `TOGGLE` 的隐藏常驻优先级会破坏“时间优先”，本轮通过统一候选比较消除了这条隐式例外；残余风险主要在于后续 bench/游戏内场景还需要继续验证多来源混合波形观测口径。[ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java#L267)

## 5. 并发冲突审查（共享状态、读改写覆盖、重入/线程边界、风险等级与建议）
- 共享状态仍集中在单个 `core` 方块实体内部：`sync` 桶、事件快照和 authority 都在 `ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent` / `ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent` 中串行读改写，本轮没有新增跨实体共享容器或静态缓存。[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L23) [ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java#L17)
