## 1. 功能与语义、架构设计（关键方法和入口）

- `X55` 通过两个 `sync_emitter -> core` 的 fan-in 结构，把“earlier high=15”与“later low=7”拆成两个独立阶段，并分别对目标高态与 later low 覆盖结果做断言，直接把“跨 tick 只认最新帧”的语义外化到游戏内 functional 用例中。[sync_later_lower_strength_overrides_earlier.json](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/tools/bench/cases/functional/x55/sync_later_lower_strength_overrides_earlier.json#L19) [sync_later_lower_strength_overrides_earlier.json](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/tools/bench/cases/functional/x55/sync_later_lower_strength_overrides_earlier.json#L76) [sync_later_lower_strength_overrides_earlier.json](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/tools/bench/cases/functional/x55/sync_later_lower_strength_overrides_earlier.json#L111)
- `X56` 使用同 tick 批量输入让 `15` 与 `7` 同时进入同一个 `sync` 帧，再用普通边 remove 作为高强度贡献失效动作，验证目标不是清零，也不是回到旧 tick，而是重算到同 tick 仍存在的低强度 `sync`。[sync_same_tick_high_removed_recompute_to_low.json](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/tools/bench/cases/functional/x56/sync_same_tick_high_removed_recompute_to_low.json#L63) [sync_same_tick_high_removed_recompute_to_low.json](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/tools/bench/cases/functional/x56/sync_same_tick_high_removed_recompute_to_low.json#L107) [sync_same_tick_high_removed_recompute_to_low.json](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/tools/bench/cases/functional/x56/sync_same_tick_high_removed_recompute_to_low.json#L119)
- 专用 suite 把这两条纯 `sync` 语义收口成独立回归入口，并固定在 `all_sync + batchWindowTicks=0` 配置下执行，避免与 mixed/restart 套件混淆。[sync-cross-tick-and-recompute-regression.json](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/tools/bench/suites/sync-cross-tick-and-recompute-regression.json#L4)
