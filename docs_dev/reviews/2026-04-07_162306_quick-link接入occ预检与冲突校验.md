## 1. 功能与语义、架构设计（关键方法和入口）
- quick-link 右键应用从原来的单段直写，调整为“预检拿 revision 基线 -> 自动续发正式 apply”的两段式协议；用户侧仍然保持一次右键交互，协议入口定义在 [QuickLinkNetwork.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetwork.java#L145) 、[QuickLinkNetwork.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetwork.java#L190) 、[QuickLinkNetwork.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetwork.java#L243)。
- 客户端在 `UseBlockCallback` 中先发送预检请求，收到基线后由主线程自动续发正式 apply，避免把 revision 真值留给客户端本地猜测，见 [QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L81) 、[QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L151) 、[QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L223)。
- 服务端在预检阶段仅做目标合法性校验并回传 revision 基线，在正式 apply 前再按目标类型执行 OCC 冲突拦截；`triggerSource` 只看 `sourceRevision`，`core` 只看 `graphRevision`，见 [QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L91) 、[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L116) 、[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L241)。

## 2. 关键数据结构和算法性能分析
- 新增的数据结构只有三类轻量 payload 和一个客户端待处理记录，全部是常数级字段，不引入集合型缓存或额外持久化结构；协议编解码仍为 O(1) 读写，见 [QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java#L107) 、[QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java#L139) 、[QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L26)。
- 服务端预检回包只读取 `LinkSavedData` 当前 `graphRevision/sourceRevision` 真值，不扫描链路集合，也不重复组装 replace 计划；真实写入仍复用既有 `QuickLinkApplyService` 闭环，见 [QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L151) 、[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L215)。
- 自动化回归新增了 payload round-trip 与冲突语义纯逻辑测试，能在不启动完整服务端上下文的前提下覆盖主要协议和判定分支，见 [QuickLinkNetworkPayloadTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadTest.java#L66) 、[QuickLinkNetworkPayloadTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadTest.java#L92) 、[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupportTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupportTest.java#L15)。

- 正式调用链现在是：客户端 `UseBlockCallback` 触发预检请求 -> 服务端校验命中节点并回传 revision 基线 -> 客户端比对待处理目标后自动续发正式 apply -> 服务端做 revision mismatch 判定 -> 通过后进入既有 quick-link apply 服务，见 [QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L81) 、[QuickLinkNetworkRegistrationSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkRegistrationSupport.java#L74) 、[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L91) 、[QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L223) 、[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L132)。

## 4. 安全、性能、兼容性、扩展性风险分析与建议
- 安全性：预检和正式 apply 都复用了同一套目标节点身份校验，必须同时满足维度、坐标、类型、序号一致才会继续执行，因此不会因为新增预检而放宽目标伪造边界，见 [QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L99) 、[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L189)。
- 性能：相比旧实现，新增的是一次常数级 RTT 与两个 `VarLong` 级别的 revision 字段，没有引入链路全集遍历或持久化读写放大；全标签自动化测试通过，说明改动没有破坏现有构建与测试基线，见 [QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java#L107) 、[QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java#L139)。
- 兼容性：本轮未改 `LinkSavedData` 存档键，也没有引入 `com.makomi.api.v1.*` 依赖；兼容边界仅在 quick-link 专用网络协议，意味着客户端和服务端需要保持同版，见 [QuickLinkNetwork.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetwork.java#L145) 、[QuickLinkNetwork.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetwork.java#L190) 、[QuickLinkNetwork.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetwork.java#L243)。
- 扩展性建议：若后续要继续把命令、bench 或编辑器入口纳入 OCC，优先继续复用当前 `LinkSavedData revision` 真值与“预检/提交”分层，而不是为每个入口再造一套版本源；当前这层拆分已经把编码、注册和冲突判定各自隔离，见 [QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java#L107) 、[QuickLinkNetworkRegistrationSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkRegistrationSupport.java#L25) 、[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L215)。

## 5. 并发冲突审查（共享状态、读改写覆盖、重入/线程边界、风险等级与建议）
- 共享状态：服务端共享真值仍然只有 `LinkSavedData` 的 `graphRevision/sourceRevision`；正式 apply 在真实写入前先用真值做 mismatch 判定，因此“预检后又被别人改过”的窗口会被显式拒绝，不再静默覆盖。风险等级评估为“中 -> 低（已显式拦截）”，见 [QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L132) 、[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L241)。
- 线程边界：客户端网络回包通过 `context.client().execute(...)` 切回主线程后才会读写 `pendingApplyBaselineRequest`，服务端接包也统一切回主线程处理，因此没有把该状态暴露给跨线程并发访问。风险等级评估为“低”，见 [QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L34) 、[QuickLinkNetworkRegistrationSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkRegistrationSupport.java#L59)。
- 重入与覆盖：客户端只保留一个待处理目标快照，旧响应只有在目标上下文完全匹配时才会续发正式 apply；这意味着“连续点不同方块”时较早的回包会被自然丢弃，不会错误覆盖到最新目标。当前风险等级评估为“低”，见 [QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L92) 、[QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L247)。
- 建议：若后续出现“同一 tick 内批量 quick-link apply”或脚本化客户端场景，再考虑把单一待处理目标升级为显式请求 ID；在当前人工交互模型下，现有“上下文匹配 + 主线程串行”已经足够。相关保护测试见 [QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupportTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupportTest.java#L15)。
