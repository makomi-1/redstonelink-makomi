# sync硬压制与跨tick共存

## 1. 功能与语义、架构设计（关键方法和入口）
- 新增配置 `crosschunk.syncCrossTickOverride`，以 `core` 端开关控制 `sync -> sync` 是“跨 tick 共存”还是“沿用最新帧覆盖”，默认关闭；配置模型、模板、解析器和默认快照已同步对齐，见 [RedstoneLinkCrossChunkConfig.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/config/RedstoneLinkCrossChunkConfig.java#L16)、[RedstoneLinkConfigTemplate.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/config/RedstoneLinkConfigTemplate.java#L287)、[RedstoneLinkCrossChunkConfigParser.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/config/RedstoneLinkCrossChunkConfigParser.java#L42)、[RedstoneLinkServerConfigSnapshot.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/config/RedstoneLinkServerConfigSnapshot.java#L92)。
- `sync` 真值从“只看最新帧”扩展为“默认按 surviving 来源聚合 max”，入口仍集中在并发桶组件内，且 `pulse/toggle` 快照不再清理 `sync` 桶；对应实现见 [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L199)、[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L403)、[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L416)、[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L443)。
- 跨类型语义改为“只要存在有效 `sync` 真值就硬压制事件类”，这一层分别在 direct/runtime/batch 派发和 `core` 本地 merged 入口都做了 guard，见 [ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java#L227)、[ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java#L382)、[ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L454)、[ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L488)、[ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L508)。
- 类间仲裁被收敛成“两段式”：先判定是否存在有效 `sync`，再决定是否进入 `pulse/toggle` 旧仲裁；`sync` 时间键只保留给自身元数据与观测使用，见 [ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java#L146)、[ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java#L217)、[ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java#L248)。
- 对外扇出去重保留 `sync` 的时间键记录，但去重条件不再因为纯 `TimeKey` 变化而重复扇出，只看 `active + power`，见 [ActivatableTargetObservationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetObservationComponent.java#L119)、[ActivatableTargetObservationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetObservationComponent.java#L132)、[ActivatableTargetObservationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetObservationComponent.java#L140)。

## 2. 关键数据结构和算法性能分析
- `syncConcurrentBuckets` / `runtimeSimulatedSyncConcurrentBuckets` 仍以 `NavigableMap<TimeKey, Map<SourceKey, SyncConcurrentEntry>>` 组织，但默认共存模式下真值重算会按“每来源取最新候选，再聚合 max”执行，而不是只读最后一个桶，见 [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L403)、[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L622)。
- 删除路径增加了带 `TimeKey + seq` 的时序保护，避免更旧的失效事件把更晚写入的同来源 `sync` 误删；关键逻辑在 [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L296) 和 [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L809)。
- `sync` 候选时间键仍从最新非空桶读取，避免共存模式下扫描后再额外排序；这部分代价是一次倒序寻桶，见 [ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java#L232)、[ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetArbitrationComponent.java#L248)。
- 持久化快照继续只写真实持久 `sync`，但在共存模式下会把 surviving 来源全集导出，避免重启后丢掉较早帧的有效贡献，见 [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L456)、[ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L839)。

## 4. 安全、性能、兼容性、扩展性风险分析与建议
- 兼容性：默认快照与 parser/template 默认值如果不一致，会导致“未写配置文件”和“显式写默认值”行为分叉；本轮已同步修正，见 [RedstoneLinkServerConfigSnapshot.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/config/RedstoneLinkServerConfigSnapshot.java#L92)、[NodeSnapshotQueryServiceTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/data/NodeSnapshotQueryServiceTest.java#L320)。建议以后新增 record 字段时统一全局检索显式构造点。
- 性能：默认共存后，`sync` 重算成本与“当前存活来源数 + 非空桶数”相关，而不再天然收敛到最新一帧；当前尚未引入硬上限，风险主要在极端大 fan-in `core`，关键落点见 [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L622)。建议后续若要控风险，优先做诊断阈值而不是默认硬裁剪。
- 行为兼容：`sync` 硬压制后，`pulse/toggle` 在 `sync` 存在期间会直接拒收，旧 mixed 用例不再保留事件回落；这是本轮刻意改变的产品语义，见 [ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java#L227)、[ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java#L382)。
- 观测兼容：`SYNC` 扇出去重不再受 `TimeKey` 变化驱动，能减少无效邻居通知，但如果以后有依赖“同功率但更新时间键也要通知”的旁路逻辑，需要单独设计新的诊断或 replay 钩子，见 [ActivatableTargetObservationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetObservationComponent.java#L119)、[LinkRedstoneDustCoreStateContractTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/block/LinkRedstoneDustCoreStateContractTest.java#L168)。

## 5. 并发冲突审查（共享状态、读改写覆盖、重入/线程边界、风险等级与建议）
- 共享状态：核心共享状态仍集中在 `core` 实体的并发桶、authority 和观测缓存上；写路径全部先回到实体内部组件，再统一重算 authority，见 [ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L661)、[ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetBlockEntity.java#L691)。当前线程边界仍是主线程实体更新，风险等级 `低`。
- 读改写覆盖：本轮最重要的冲突防护是带时间键的 `removeSyncConcurrentSource`，可以阻止更旧 invalidation/remove 覆盖更晚 `sync` upsert，见 [ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L296)、[ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetConcurrentBucketComponent.java#L809)。这把“共享状态被旧事件误清空”的风险从 `中` 降到了 `低`。
- 重入与重复通知：`sync` 存在时 direct/batch/local merged 入口都会拒绝事件写入，同时观察层只在 `active + power` 变化时重新扇出，减少了同 tick/跨 tick 重入噪声，见 [ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java#L227)、[ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetDispatchSupport.java#L382)、[ActivatableTargetObservationComponent.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/block/entity/ActivatableTargetObservationComponent.java#L132)。
- 建议：后续若引入“每个 `core` 最大活跃 sync 来源数”之类容量阈值，需要把裁剪动作也纳入这套时间键比较保护，否则会重新引入“新值被旧清理覆盖”的并发式语义风险。
