## 2. 关键数据结构和算法性能分析

- 服务端新增 `VisualizedObjectRevisionBaseline`，并把它嵌入 `VisualizedObjectSnapshot`。这样首包与后续增量刷新都能共享同一份对象级版本基线，避免客户端自行推导图真值。[QuickLinkVisualizationSnapshotService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/QuickLinkVisualizationSnapshotService.java#L298) [QuickLinkVisualizationSnapshotService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/QuickLinkVisualizationSnapshotService.java#L312)
- 服务端增量比较只看对象真正相关的 `sourceRevision/coreRevision`，显式避开全局 `graphRevision` 作为刷新触发条件，从而避免任意拓扑改动都把所有显示对象一起刷一遍；`runtimeNodeVersion` 只在运行时上下线/坐标变化时触发一轮批量复核。[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L482)
- 客户端缓存仍是 `LinkedHashMap<VisualizedObjectKey, VisualizedObjectState>`，但每个状态现在额外持有本地 revision 基线和全局 `visualizedRuntimeNodeVersion`；轮询请求只导出对象键与基线， steady-state 下是小包对比，不是全量快照重拉。[QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L113) [QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L265) [QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L1262)
- 编解码层将增量刷新拆成“对象基线列表 + upsert 列表 + removal 列表”，用结构化 payload 保持网络体积与语义边界清晰。[QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java#L163) [QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java#L279) [QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java#L310)

## 4. 安全、性能、兼容性、扩展性风险分析与建议

- 性能风险：`runtimeNodeVersion` 是全局版本，只要任意节点上下线就会触发当前显示对象的一轮批量复核。当前通过 10 tick 轮询和对象级 upsert 限制了成本，但在高频上下线场景下仍会放大刷新面。[QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L32) [QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L514)
- 兼容性边界：本轮只保证连接与运行时位置变化自动刷新，alias-only 变化仍不会主动推送到第三形态显示，这是有意留白；若后续要补，建议引入独立显示文本版本而不是复用 `graphRevision`。[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L482)
- 安全与语义边界：服务端刷新请求仍基于显示资格校验，不允许未佩戴眼镜时持续取图；tooltip 文案变化仅影响显示顺序，不改实际交互逻辑。[QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L194) [LinkerItem.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/item/LinkerItem.java#L251)
- 回归覆盖：已补充 payload 编解码与第三形态缓存测试样本，确保新增 baseline 字段不会把既有结构打断。[QuickLinkOutlineRendererTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRendererTest.java#L58) [QuickLinkNetworkPayloadTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadTest.java#L132)

## 5. 并发冲突审查（共享状态、读改写覆盖、重入/线程边界、风险等级与建议）

- 客户端共享状态主要是 `visualizedObjects` 与 `visualizedRuntimeNodeVersion`。接包更新通过 `context.client().execute(...)` 切回客户端线程，轮询发送和渲染读取也都发生在客户端线程，因此没有新增跨线程并发读写风险，风险等级低。[QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L53) [QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/network/QuickLinkNetworkClientHandlerSupport.java#L58) [QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L113)
- 服务端共享状态读取集中在 `LinkSavedData` 和图查询服务，刷新请求统一通过 `player.server.execute(...)` 回到主线程处理，不会与服务端图写入链形成异步竞争，风险等级低。[QuickLinkNetworkRegistrationSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkRegistrationSupport.java#L112) [QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkServerHandlerSupport.java#L194)
- 读改写覆盖风险主要在“删除后又 upsert 同一对象”的顺序。本轮在客户端刷新应用时先删后写，能确保同一回包内最终状态以最新 upsert 为准，避免顺序覆盖错误，风险等级中低。[QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRenderer.java#L324)
