## 2. 关键数据结构和算法性能分析

- 本次没有新增数据结构，仍只使用 `TitleStyle` 传递正文色与描边色，复杂度和内存占用基本不变。[GuiTitleRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiTitleRenderSupport.java#L74)

## 3. 数据流或调用链

1. 各 GUI 在头部渲染阶段调用 `GuiHeaderRenderSupport.drawCenteredHeader(...)`，根据是否有标题/副标题转发到 `GuiTitleRenderSupport`。[GuiHeaderRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiHeaderRenderSupport.java#L61) [GuiHeaderRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiHeaderRenderSupport.java#L70) [GuiHeaderRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiHeaderRenderSupport.java#L73)
2. `GuiTitleRenderSupport` 先解析视觉顺序文本和居中左边界，再把正文色、描边色、矩阵和缓冲区传给原版 `drawInBatch8xOutline(...)`。[GuiTitleRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiTitleRenderSupport.java#L49) [GuiTitleRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiTitleRenderSupport.java#L50) [GuiTitleRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiTitleRenderSupport.java#L57)
3. 副标题描边色仍通过 `TitleStyle.secondary(...) -> deriveOutlineColor(...)` 生成，因此调用链变化只发生在最终渲染出口，不影响主题色推导链路。[GuiTitleRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiTitleRenderSupport.java#L33) [GuiTitleRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiTitleRenderSupport.java#L79) [GuiTitleRenderSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/client/java/com/makomi/client/screen/GuiTitleRenderSupport.java#L82)
