# 第三形态增量同步扩展时同步核对revision签名与测试样例

## 阻塞点

- 在第三形态显示对象补 revision 基线时，`LinkSavedData.sourceRevision` 不是单参数接口，而是 `sourceRevision(LinkNodeType, long)`。直接按 `serial` 读取会在主源码编译阶段失败。[LinkSavedData.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/LinkSavedData.java#L211) [QuickLinkVisualizationSnapshotService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/QuickLinkVisualizationSnapshotService.java#L235)
- 扩展 `QuickLinkVisualizeSnapshotPayload` 字段后，业务代码先编译通过，但测试里的手工构造样本仍停留在旧签名，导致全量回归在 `compileTestJava` 阶段失败。[QuickLinkNetwork.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetwork.java#L264) [QuickLinkOutlineRendererTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRendererTest.java#L58) [QuickLinkNetworkPayloadTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadTest.java#L132)

## 解决方法

- 读取 `triggerSource` 侧 revision 时，统一显式传入 `LinkNodeType.TRIGGER_SOURCE`，不要假设 `sourceRevision` 会按序号自动推导节点类型；这样能保持 `triggerSource/core` 方向语义明确，也和现有 OCC 基线读取方式一致。[QuickLinkVisualizationSnapshotService.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/data/QuickLinkVisualizationSnapshotService.java#L243)
- 每次给网络 record 增字段后，除了更新编解码实现，还要同步搜索测试中所有直接 `new Payload(...)` 的样本构造器，并补上对应断言字段，否则 `compileJava` 通过不代表全量回归可过。[QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/main/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadSupport.java#L163) [QuickLinkOutlineRendererTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/client/render/QuickLinkOutlineRendererTest.java#L58) [QuickLinkNetworkPayloadTest.java](/d:/OpenProjects/RedstoneLink/rl-release-no-mixin/src/test/java/com/makomi/network/QuickLinkNetworkPayloadTest.java#L132)
